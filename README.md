 About SnapSync

SnapSync is a web-based application designed to bridge the distance between couples and long-distance partners (LDRs). It provides an interactive digital space where couples can spend quality time together through real-time video calls, synchronized virtual photobooth sessions, mini-games, and instant messaging.

Our goal is to make long-distance relationships feel closer by turning ordinary online interactions into meaningful shared experiences.

Key Features
 Real-Time Video Calls — Connect face-to-face with your partner through live video and voice communication.
 Synchronized LDR Photobooth — Capture photos together virtually with countdown timers, custom filters, and interactive photo strip templates.
Mini-Games — Enjoy interactive games designed to help couples have fun and bond while spending time together.
Instant Messaging — Send messages and communicate with your partner during calls and photobooth sessions.
User Authentication & Profiles — Manage accounts and personalized profiles using Supabase authentication and database services.
Days Together Counter — Celebrate your relationship by tracking the days since your anniversary.
Dual Time Zones — View your local time and your partner's time to make staying connected across countries easier.
Next Meet-Up Countdown — Count down the days until your next visit and look forward to seeing each other again.
Note: The availability of individual features depends on the current implementation of the live application.
